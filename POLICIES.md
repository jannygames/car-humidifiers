# DriveMist store policies — drafts

Paste these into **Shopify admin → Settings → Policies**. Each one gets its own field, and
Shopify publishes them at `/policies/…` automatically. The DriveMist footer reads
`shop.policies` directly, so **the moment you save a policy it appears in the footer and in the
bottom bar** — there is no menu to wire up.

> **These are drafts, not legal advice.** Fill in every `[BRACKETED]` placeholder and have
> someone qualified review them before you take real orders — especially the governing-law
> clause and the statutory EU withdrawal rights.

Keep them consistent with what the storefront says: **free tracked shipping**, dispatch in
**1–2 working days**, delivery in **7–14 working days**, **30-day** returns, contact
**info@drivemist.com**. If your supplier's real delivery times differ, change them here *and*
in the theme (product page "Estimated delivery" min/max days, FAQ answers).

---

## Putting these on their own pages

The **`page.policy`** theme template renders policies published as regular pages
(Online Store → Pages → Add page → **Theme template: policy**).

It renders a DriveMist page header, a sticky "On this page" contents list built automatically
from the page's **Heading 2** blocks, the policy body in the site's prose styles, a
"Questions about this policy?" card, and the trust strip.

1. **Add page** → title it (Privacy policy / Shipping policy / Returns policy / Terms of service).
2. Use these handles so the footer links work: `shipping-policy`, `returns-policy`,
   `privacy-policy`, `terms-of-service`.
3. Paste the body below and format each bold sub-head as **Heading 2**.
4. Pick **policy** in the Theme template dropdown, save.

## Shipping policy

**Where we ship**
We ship to the countries listed at checkout. If your country is not listed, email
info@drivemist.com and we will check whether we can deliver to you.

**Processing time**
Orders are processed within 1–2 working days. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays are
processed on the next working day.

**Delivery time and cost**
Tracked delivery is free on every order. Most parcels arrive within 7–14 working days of
dispatch. The estimated delivery date is shown at checkout. Delivery times are estimates, not
guarantees, and can be affected by carrier delays, customs and peak seasons.

**Tracking**
As soon as your order ships we email you a tracking link. You can also follow your parcel on the
[Track your order](/pages/track-your-order) page.

**Duties and import taxes**
Prices include VAT where applicable. For deliveries outside the EU, local duties or taxes may
apply and are the responsibility of the recipient.

**Incorrect addresses**
Please check your address carefully. If a parcel is returned to us because of an incorrect or
incomplete address, we can re-send it once the additional shipping cost is paid.

**Lost or damaged parcels**
If your tracking has not updated for 10 working days, or your model arrives damaged, email us
with your order number (and photos of any damage) within 7 days of delivery. We will send a
replacement or a full refund.

## Refund policy

**30-day returns**
You can return an unused car humidifier in its original packaging within 30 days of delivery.
This is in addition to your statutory 14-day right of withdrawal in the EU.

**How to return something**
Email info@drivemist.com with your order number and the item you want to return. We will reply
with the return address and instructions. Please do not send items back without contacting us
first.

**Return shipping**
Return shipping costs are paid by the customer unless the item is faulty or we sent the wrong
item.

**Refunds**
Once your return arrives and we have checked it, we refund the price of the item to your
original payment method within 14 days. We will email you when the refund is issued.

**What we cannot accept**
Items that have been used with oils or fragrances, damaged by misuse, or returned without their
original packaging, unless they are faulty.

**Faulty items**
If your humidifier stops working because of a manufacturing defect, contact us. Your statutory
2-year legal guarantee applies, and we will repair, replace or refund it.

**Cancellations**
You can cancel free of charge until your order has shipped. Email us as soon as possible with
your order number.
