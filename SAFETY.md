```html
<p>DriveMist car humidifiers are low-voltage USB devices designed for cars, desks and bedside
tables. A few simple habits keep them safe, clean and misting strongly for years.</p>

<h2>Placement in the car</h2>

<ul>
<li>Use it in a cup holder or on a flat, non-slip surface where it cannot slide or tip over.</li>
<li>Never place it where it could block your view of the road, your mirrors, your controls or
an airbag.</li>
<li>Point the exhausts away from screens, buttons and other electronics.</li>
<li>Switch it on, fill it and adjust it only while the car is parked. Never handle it while
driving.</li>
</ul>

<h2>What goes in the tank</h2>

<ul>
<li>Use clean tap or distilled water at room temperature. Never use hot water.</li>
<li>Fill only up to the maximum line.</li>
<li>For fragrance, add 2–3 drops of water-soluble essential oil or diffuser fragrance. Thick,
oil-only perfumes can clog the atomiser and are not covered by the guarantee.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Power</h2>

<ul>
<li>Use the included USB cable with a 5 V USB port: your car's USB socket, a laptop, a power
bank or a standard phone charger.</li>
<li>Unplug it before filling, emptying or cleaning.</li>
<li>Do not use it with a damaged cable.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Cleaning and care</h2>

<ul>
<li>Empty the tank after each use and let it dry with the lid open.</li>
<li>Once a week, clean the atomiser gently with a cotton bud and a little white vinegar, then
rinse with clean water.</li>
<li>Never submerge the model in water or put it in a dishwasher.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Children and pets</h2>

<p>DriveMist humidifiers are decorative devices, not toys. Keep them out of reach of small
children and pets, and supervise older children when they use one as a night light.</p>

<h2>Hot cars and cold weather</h2>

<p>Don't leave water in the tank in a parked car in freezing weather or in direct summer sun.
Empty it when you leave the car for long periods.</p>
```
